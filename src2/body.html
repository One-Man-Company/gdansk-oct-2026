Train route, step by step
  1. Arrivals → Gdańsk Port Lotniczy station: covered walkway, ~5 min.
  2. Ticket in Jakdojade/GoPay or the machine: 4.80 zł single, 6 zł for the 75-min transfer ticket, 18 zł for 24 h.
  3. Any SKM/PKM towards Gdańsk Główny, ~25 min (some need a change at Wrzeszcz — same platform).
  4. Tram 10 towards Siedlce/Emaus, 4 stops to Zakopiańska, then 300 m walk.
  5. Validate on boarding. Inspectors fine on the spot (~150 zł).

Etiquette & safety

  • Safe city; watch pockets on trams at night.
  • Open bottles not allowed on trams — fine on the embankment.
  • Clubs: no flip-flops or sports shorts.
  • Quiet in churches; shoulders covered.
  • No smoking indoors — most bars have heated gardens.

Three things that will go wrong

  1. You will be colder than you think. 9 °C with 40 km/h wind feels like 2 °C. Waterproof shell + beanie; keep bar-to-bar hops under 10 minutes.
  2. Sunday afternoon will be dead outdoors. Book the museum slot and the indoor activity on Saturday night — axe and shooting need 24 h notice.
  3. You will cut it fine for the flight. Leave the flat at 20:00, eat before 19:45, bags packed by 18:00.
